# ASI_Controller
This is my senior project for mechatronics. We have developed a first of a kind ultra low power imager designed to study aurora.

The research system is an all-sky imager intended to operate in the deep field regions of Antarctica, where power availability is extremely limited and environmental conditions are severe. 

The project focuses on reliable long-duration aurora observations while maintaining ultra-low power consumption across sensing, capture, and control subsystems.
